Privacy Policy

For the Crosspost app · Effective 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crosspost, an app made and operated by Millquas Productions ("we", "us"), handles information. Crosspost is an internal tool used only by Millquas Productions to publish its own videos and photos to its own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect personal information from anyone else.

1. Who we are

Millquas Productions is a film and video production company. Crosspost is our in-house app for scheduling and publishing our own content to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest. This policy applies to Crosspost and to this website, millquasproductions.github.io.

2. Information we access

Crosspost only connects to social media accounts owned by Millquas Productions, and only after we sign in and approve access. From Pinterest, Crosspost accesses:

  • Account name, to confirm which of our accounts is connected.
  • Boards, to list our boards and create new ones, so we can choose where each Pin goes.
  • Pins, to create Pins from our own videos and photos, with the title, description and link we write.

Crosspost does not access other users' Pins, profiles, followers, messages, contacts or analytics.

3. How we use information

We use this access only to publish our own content to our own accounts at the times we choose. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, marketing to others or any other purpose.

4. How information is stored

Crosspost runs on a Millquas Productions computer. The sign-in tokens issued by Pinterest and other platforms, our account name, our chosen board, and our posting schedule are stored in files on that computer only. We do not run any server or database for Crosspost, and this information is not uploaded anywhere else. Access to the computer is limited to Millquas Productions.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone. Crosspost sends content only to the platform it is publishing to: a Pin is sent to Pinterest and nowhere else.

6. Cookies and tracking

Crosspost and this website use no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

7. Keeping and deleting information

We keep sign-in tokens only while Crosspost is connected to an account. To disconnect Pinterest, we remove Crosspost's access in Pinterest's settings and delete the saved Pinterest file from Crosspost, which removes all Pinterest information Crosspost holds. Pins we have published stay on Pinterest until we delete them there.

8. Children

Crosspost is an internal business tool. It is not directed at children and does not collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Crosspost handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to Millquas Productions through our Pinterest account or our GitHub profile at github.com/millquasproductions.
